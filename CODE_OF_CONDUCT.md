# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project welcomes contributions and discussion from anyone, regardless of experience level,
background, or identity. We're committed to keeping issues, pull requests, and any other project
spaces harassment-free and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on the code/idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith, especially in async, text-only discussion where tone is easy to misread.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Discriminatory jokes, language, or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or reviews.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits/comments) and in any other setting where an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening a GitHub issue on this repository. Reports will be reviewed
and may result in a warning, temporary block, or permanent ban from project spaces at the
maintainer's discretion, depending on severity.

If the report concerns a maintainer or is otherwise sensitive to raise in a public issue, note that
in the issue and ask for a private follow-up.
